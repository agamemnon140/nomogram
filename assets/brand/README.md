# Ícone Pace

Imagem original: `pace-master.png`, criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens (image_gen). O desenho usa fundo laranja opaco, dois arcos brancos e uma agulha, sem texto. O símbolo fica dentro da área segura para máscaras de instalação. A imagem original é mantida aqui; as reduções PNG são geradas por Sharp com `npm run icons`.

Arquivos usados pelo aplicativo: `public/pace-icon-192.png`, `public/pace-icon-512.png`, `public/pace-apple-touch-icon.png`, `public/pace-favicon-32.png` e `public/pace-favicon-48.png`.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production app icon for Pace Nomogram, a pace/speed conversion running app. Create one square 1024x1024 finished icon image, no mockup. Full-bleed opaque warm burnt-orange background (#C74E28) with very subtle tonal depth. In the center, a crisp warm-white instrument mark: two concentric open circular gauge arcs with the opening at the bottom, a small round central pivot, and a single bold elegant needle pointing up-right at about 45 degrees. The outer arc has only five or six clean substantial tick marks, the inner arc is thinner and smooth. The design evokes the app's circular nomogram and forward movement, sophisticated minimalist sporting instrument, balanced geometry, precise edges, strong legibility at tiny home-screen sizes. Keep the entire white symbol inside the central 68 percent of the canvas so it remains intact under circular or rounded-square OS masks. The background goes all the way to all four square corners: do not draw rounded outer corners. No text, numbers, letters, gradients on the white mark, bevels, metallic effects, shadows outside the icon, extra objects, borders, watermark, or presentation frame.
```
